# For Locals – Join BitcoinSranan

BitcoinSranan is about making Bitcoin part of everyday life in Suriname.  
This page is your guide to getting started and joining the community.

---

## 🚀 Why join?
- Pay and receive money instantly, without banks or intermediaries
- Protect your savings against inflation
- Connect with local shops and neighbors who already use Bitcoin
- Be part of building Suriname’s first Bitcoin circular economy

---

## 📱 Step 1: Get a Wallet
Download a mobile Bitcoin wallet to start:
- Blink Wallet (easy and beginner-friendly)
- Phoenix Wallet (non-custodial, not yor keys not your coins)

👉 Once [installed](), you’ll have your own Bitcoin address and Lightning capability.

---

## 💳 Step 2: Try a BoltCard
With a **BoltCard** you can pay by simply tapping – just like a bank card, but with Bitcoin.  
We provide NFC cards locally and help you set them up with **LNbits** for maximum privacy.

👉 Setup at work

## 🛒 Step 3: Make Your First Payment
- Visit a shop that accepts Bitcoin
- Scan the QR code or tap your BoltCard
- Experience instant, borderless payment

---

## 👥 Step 4: Join the Community
- Attend workshops and meetups at the **Community Hub** (old Surinamese House)
- Learn from others and share your experience
- Help onboard new shops and neighbors
