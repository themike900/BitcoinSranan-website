## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
**Is Bitcoin safe?**  
Yes – Bitcoin transactions are secured by cryptography and the Lightning Network enables instant payments.

**Do I need internet to use Bitcoin?**  
Yes, but payments are lightweight and work even on mobile data.

**Can shops set prices in Surinamese Dollars?**  
Yes – point-of-sale apps automatically convert SRD to Bitcoin at the current rate.

**What if I lose my phone?**  
Your wallet can be restored with a backup phrase. Always write it down and keep it safe.


## 📖 Glossary
- **Bitcoin** → A decentralized digital currency without banks or intermediaries
- **Lightning Network** → A fast payment layer built on Bitcoin
- **NFC-card** → NFC card for tap-to-pay Bitcoin transactions
- **BTCPayServer** → Open-source payment processor for donations and merchant payments
- **Circular Economy** → A system where Bitcoin is earned and spent locally, creating self-sustaining value
